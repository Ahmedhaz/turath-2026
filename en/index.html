Why this canon

Today’s reader lives inside a crisis made of three forces: the flood of data, the screens of consumption, and the reach of artificial intelligence. He is offered two failing options: a frozen reading of medieval texts that cannot bring the remedy to today’s ailment, or imported self-help emptied of depth.

This canon breaks that choice. It is neither commentary nor dry simplification but a structural re-derivation: the master’s architecture and method are kept, and the remedy is applied to the ailments of the twenty-first century.

  1. In the author’s voiceAl-Ghazālī speaks for himself; he is not paraphrased.
  2. The structure is keptThe original’s order and method are the spine.
  3. Sources verifiedEvery chapter closes with its source notes.
  4. A declared ceilingNo fatwa, no clinical diagnosis, no judgement of anyone.
