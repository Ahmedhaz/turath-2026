About

What this canon is, and how it was written

The idea

The Canon for Resuming the Intellectual and Spiritual Heritage is a single project of 14 books re-derived from the classics of the Islamic tradition. It was born from one sentence: the heritage holds the cure, and it has lost the index. The books here are not a set placed side by side but one body of knowledge with a map.

The method

  • Re-derivation, not commentary: each book is written in its author’s own voice, keeping the original’s order and method, and applying its remedy to the ailments of the age of screens and artificial intelligence.
  • A fixed chapter structure: diagnosis of today’s crisis, the source made clear, the inner anatomy, a step-by-step practice, the witnesses, and a scale for self-review.
  • Verification: verses, hadith and reports are checked, and their sources are listed at the end of each chapter.
  • The unifying framework: every unit is tagged with the ailments it treats and the layer it works on, so it can be found from the map.

The ceiling

These are chapters of self-cultivation, formation and thought. They are not fatwas, not clinical diagnosis, not medical prescription, and never a judgement of anyone before God. Anyone in serious distress belongs with qualified professional help.

Language

The books are written in Arabic. The English pages of this site describe the project, the framework and each book, and link to the Arabic chapters.

Direction

The project is directed by Ahmed Abu Elhaz.
