From the canon to an ordinary day

A Wider Life

“You are not short of information. You are not short of advice. You are short of structure.”

A book for someone whose life has got narrow, and who cannot say when it happened. One complete map: what you are made of, what narrows your life, what its opposite is, and how to walk through a real day with work and children and a phone that will not stay quiet. And an app that walks the journey with you.

The editions

One book, five editions

The structure is the same in all of them: six layers, twenty two ailments and their opposites, four circles, a day and a week. What changes is who the book speaks to, and in what language.

Religious edition · Arabic, classical and colloquial

A Wider Life

For the reader who wants the source in view

The same structure and the same map, with verses and hadith shown in the text. Two versions written from separate sources, classical and colloquial, neither a translation of the other. Every text in it was checked against three sources; what could not be confirmed was left out.

Global edition · Arabic

A Wider Life

For anyone

The edition that speaks to anyone, with no religious vocabulary and no named source. Written in Egyptian colloquial Arabic, with scenes from a real day in 2026 at the heart of each chapter: nameless people living the state before the rule is named. Its fifth part is the app.

Global edition · English

A Wider Life

Written from the structure, not translated

The same book in English, written straight from the structure, with scenes that are English from the start. The week is re-derived for its reader’s rhythm, the spelling is British, and there is no religious content in it.

Arabic · The structure of a founder in the age of AI

A Wider Life for Founders

For the one building a company while his life narrows

The same map laid over a founder’s life: burnout is dust on the mirror, runway is a resident fear, and the four circles become yourself, your home, your team and your investors. The scenes come from startups, in the words founders actually use.

English · The structure of a founder in the age of AI

A Wider Life for Founders

Newly written, not translated

Written from two sources for every chapter: the general English edition for voice and argument, the Arabic founders’ edition for its scenes. There is no religious content: every moment of prayer in the Arabic became an equivalent moment in a founder’s life.

The dose ladder

Full doseWhat you hope to do
HalfAn ordinary day
SmallestThe same act, smaller; not an easier one

Pressed “Smaller”? The dose drops a step at once. A day went empty? It drops silently, no message. Every new week it returns to full. Silence means “the dose was too big”, never “you failed”.

And that is on purpose

What is not in the app

The app is built for you to need it less, not to keep you in it. Its success is the day you draw your week sheet by hand without missing a field.

  • No streak to break
  • No notifications pulling at you
  • No sharing, no comments
  • No box for failure
  • No reading ahead
  • No scores, no completion rate

“One promise only: that your life widens to hold what is in it. And that is enough.”
