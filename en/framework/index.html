Axis one

Structure · What is a human being?

Six layers. Diagnosis descends from symptom to root; the remedy climbs back and is carried out in body and action.

Axis two

The path · How does one move?

Seven obstacles, grounded in The Worshippers’ Path. One stands at a single station and works on it; the whole map is never thrown at once.
