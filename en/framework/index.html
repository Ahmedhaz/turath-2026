Beneath the canon

The two machines · where the framework becomes work

Two machines were born from the canon: one for the heart, working on the poles, the path and time; one for action, reasoning to its rulings. The books are the reference, the framework is the map, and the machines walk on it.

The companion machine

Moried

A companion in refining the self, neither a sheikh nor a mufti.

It questions gently, with al-Muḥāsibī’s probes and al-Ghazālī’s tests, until the ailment shows; then it sets one small practice by the opposite and walks with the person day by day. Its memory is encrypted so only its owner can read it, and every conversation closes on mercy.

Reads from the canon
The forty books of the Iḥyāʾ, the Ḥikam, and The Worshippers’ Path.
Its place in the framework
  • The poles: twenty-two ailments, remedy by the opposite
  • The path: repentance and the obstacles
  • Time: the daily watch
  • Structure: from state to inner self
Its ceiling
No fatwa, no clinical diagnosis; a crisis is handed at once to a human and a helpline.

Trial version · its method was approved by a scholar; the psychiatrist’s review is not yet complete.

The Ghazālī project · the tool sciences

Al-Mustadil

A machine of legal reasoning that knows its limit.

It first frames the question and exposes a false premise if there is one, then gathers the evidence from a verified corpus of texts and the resolutions of councils and fatwa bodies, and writes the ruling with its reason, its basis and the disagreement, or holds back when the disagreement is real.

Reads from the canon
The tool sciences: the principles and maxims of jurisprudence, and the aims of the Law as in al-Muwāfaqāt.
Its place in the framework
  • Structure: the layer of action, the ruling on what the hand does
  • The half where Moried stops: the legal question belongs here
Its ceiling
Not a mufti but a tool in a jurist’s hand; every ruling is reviewed by a jurist, and what he approves is kept under his name.

Under juristic review · fifty benchmark questions approved by a jurist; measurement continues.
